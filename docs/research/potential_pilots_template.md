# Potential Pilot Organizations – Template

**Purpose**: Use as starting point for outreach list (6–8 targets). Fill with real contacts from your network. Prioritize mix: public sector, large private, mid-sized Polish company. Do not publish real names without permission.

## Categories & Example Targets (anonymized / illustrative)

### Public Sector / Government
- Large ministry or central agency procurement department (high PZP EU exposure)
- Regional authority or large city hall (mix of national and EU thresholds)
- State-owned enterprise in infrastructure/energy

### Large Private Corporation
- Manufacturing / automotive (heavy Direct + Upstream spend)
- Retail / logistics group (mix of Direct/Indirect, time-sensitive)
- Financial services or telecom (high Indirect, complex contracts)

### Mid-sized Polish Company
- Production / food processing company (CAPEX + operational)
- Tech or services firm scaling procurement
- Construction / engineering (project-based, high renegotiation exposure)

## Selection Criteria (from plan)
- Recent high-value purchases (>1-2M PLN) in last 18-24 months
- Mix of rigid vs. more flexible approaches in practice
- Willingness for 2-4h confidential pilot (process mapping + data)
- Different 2×2 quadrants represented across cases
