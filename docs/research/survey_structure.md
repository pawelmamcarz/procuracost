# Survey Structure – ProcuraCost Empirical Study (Draft v0.9)

**Target completion time:** 12–15 minutes  
**Target respondents:** Procurement professionals (buyers, category managers, procurement leaders, CFOs with procurement oversight)

---

## Module A: Respondent & Organization Profile (5 questions)

1. **Role** (single choice)
   - Procurement specialist / Buyer
   - Category / Sourcing Manager
   - Head of Procurement / CPO
   - Finance / Controlling with procurement oversight
   - Other (open)

2. **Organization type** (single choice)
   - Public sector / Government
   - State-owned enterprise
   - Large private corporation (>500 employees)
   - Mid-sized company (50–500 employees)
   - Other

3. **Approximate annual procurement spend** (bands)
   - < 50 mln PLN
   - 50–200 mln PLN
   - 200–500 mln PLN
   - 500 mln – 2 mld PLN
   - > 2 mld PLN

4. **% of spend classified as Direct vs Indirect** (slider or two numeric fields that must sum to 100%)

5. **Maturity of digital procurement systems** (1–5 scale)
   - Mostly manual (Excel, email, paper)
   - Basic e-procurement / sourcing tool
   - Partial ERP coverage
   - End-to-end integrated platform (e.g. Ariba, Coupa, SAP full)

---

## Module B: Time Allocation by Role and Step (Core Module – Most Important)

**Instruction:** Think about a **typical purchase** in the following two categories. Estimate average hours spent by each role across the main steps of the process.

**Category 1:** Strategic / high-value Direct spend (e.g. production components, critical services, CAPEX > threshold)

**Category 2:** Operational / Indirect spend (e.g. office supplies, maintenance services, standard IT)

For each category, respondents fill a matrix:

**Steps (rows):**
- Needs definition & budget approval
- Specification / tender documentation
- Supplier identification & market approach
- Bid evaluation / negotiation
- Legal / compliance review
- Approval & contract signing
- Implementation / onboarding

**Roles (columns):**
- Requestor / Business owner
- Buyer / Procurement specialist
- Legal
- Finance / Controlling
- Manager (procurement or business)
- Executive / Board level

Respondents enter estimated hours per role per step (or "0" if negligible).

This module directly feeds the staff cost component of the model.

---

## Module C: Perceived Delay Costs

1. For a purchase of ~X PLN (respondent fills or we provide bands), what is the approximate daily cost to the organization if the purchase is delayed by one month? (open numeric or bands)

2. How often do delays in procurement cause measurable business impact in your organization? (frequency scale)

3. In which categories are delay costs highest? (multi-select with Direct/Indirect and Upstream/Downstream framing)

---

## Module D: Renegotiation Experience

1. In the last 3 years, what percentage of contracts above [threshold] required significant renegotiation or amendment after signing? (percentage or bands)

2. What were the main drivers of renegotiation? (multiple choice + open)

3. Do you observe differences in renegotiation rates between highly formal/rigid processes vs more flexible policy-driven approaches? (qualitative + rough estimate)

---

## Module E: Bypass Behavior

1. How frequently do procurement teams in your organization use informal or accelerated paths that bypass standard procedures? (scale: Never – Very frequently)

2. In which situations does this happen most often? (use 2×2 framing: Direct/Indirect × Upstream/Downstream)

3. What are the most common reasons for bypass? (rank top 3 from list: time pressure, supplier relationship, market opportunity, internal stakeholder pressure, etc.)

4. How visible / documented are these bypasses to auditors and management?

---

## Optional Module F: Technology & Governance

(Only shown if respondent indicated high digital maturity)

- To what extent do your systems automatically enforce policy constraints without requiring procedural checkpoints?
- How much of traditional "procedure compliance" work has been replaced or reduced by technology in the last 5 years?

---

## Closing

- Open comment box
- Option to be contacted for follow-up interview
- Consent for anonymized data use in research

**Next steps for this template:**
