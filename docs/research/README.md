# Research materials for model 2.3.0

The active research layer is intentionally small:

- [`RESEARCH.md`](../../RESEARCH.md) is the current working paper, evidence audit
  and bibliography.
- [`MODEL_PARAMETERS.md`](../MODEL_PARAMETERS.md) is the active parameter,
  legal-boundary and evidence-status contract.
- [`model_specification_draft.md`](model_specification_draft.md) is the compact
  implementation specification.
- [`CALIBRATION_BENCHMARKS.md`](CALIBRATION_BENCHMARKS.md) records the historical
  calibration reconciliation and its transfer limits.
- [`../supervisor/README.md`](../supervisor/README.md) is the current supervisor
  package.

Model 2.3.0 separates legal and governance boundary, purchase archetype,
procurement workflow design, purchase execution channel, system support and
contract design. Its ten reference scenarios are structured starting points;
their economic values are not observed outcomes. Official cases support mechanisms only. Practitioner
material, including [Procurement&Beyond episode 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KYUdTLlvvg),
may inform questions and hypotheses but cannot set model parameters.

The active replication contract and diagnostic commands are documented in
[`../../replication/README.md`](../../replication/README.md). Reproduction proves
code-path consistency, not empirical validity.

Protocols, surveys, preregistration templates, pitches and empirical plans
created for model 1.0 to 1.2 remain under
[`../archive/model-1.x/research/`](../archive/model-1.x/research/). They are
historical provenance and must be redesigned before new data collection. The
model 2.2.2 computational snapshot is preserved separately under
[`../../replication/archive/model-2.2.2/`](../../replication/archive/model-2.2.2/).
