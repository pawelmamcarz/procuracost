## Publication contract

Every new draft must:

1. compare `formalSequential` and `adaptiveCompliant` under the same legal and
   governance boundary;
2. separate procedure family, purchase archetype, procurement workflow design,
   execution channel, system support, contract design and organisational
   implementation readiness;
3. use the identity `deltaCost = formalSequential - adaptiveCompliant` without
   constraining its sign;
4. report declared low, central and high scenario values as ranges, not
   confidence intervals;
5. keep mandatory legal waits fixed, sourced and identical in both
   alternatives;
6. distinguish internal illustrative allocations, retained assumptions, user
   inputs, legal rules, empirical anchors, official cases, practitioner
   observations and research hypotheses;
7. use Szucs only for the bounded competition-transfer stress where market
   access differs;
8. keep contract-amendment and TCO differentials at zero in native starting
   scenarios and disclose informal bypass as non-monetised;
9. describe suitability without scoring, ranking or recommendation and keep
   readiness outside the cost model;
10. treat official cases as evidence of mechanisms, not ProcuraCost effect
    sizes;
11. use Procurement&Beyond episode 8 only for question design and hypothesis
    generation;
12. state that Bielik may structure data for review while the deterministic
    model performs the calculation.

## Required contrast in examples

Where a draft explains adaptive work, it must show both conditions.

Mechanisms can be investigated for ERP discovery, logistics service redesign,
public IT with preliminary market consultation and discovery or co-design.
Discovery can add time and effort because learning is work.

A stable standard service may have no separate workflow mechanism, although
its starting scenario separately declares a competition difference. Catalogue
call-off and MRP release use identical maps and no competition difference as
neutral controls. Their equal result must not be converted into a
recommendation.

English drafts use British spelling. Polish drafts use professional procurement
terminology from `CLAUDE_DESIGN.md` and reserve `Zamawiający` for the PZP
context.
