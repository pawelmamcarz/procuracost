# Shared foundation: model 2.3.0

**Status:** binding conceptual and quantitative contract for the active doctoral
article cycle.

Drafts with a superseded banner are historical. Model 2.2.2 may be cited when a
paper describes its audit or correction, but its optimiser, path profiles and
threshold maps are not current model claims.

## Central claim

A legal and governance boundary defines what is admissible. A procurement
workflow design specifies how authorised work proceeds inside that boundary.

The comparison between a formal/sequential and an adaptive/compliant design is
conditional. A more adaptive design may reduce queues or permit earlier
learning. It may also add discovery work, role effort or competition exposure.
A more prescribed design may protect access, documentation and control. Which
cost is larger is a question for declared inputs and empirical study, not a
premise.

## Domain contract

For the same purchase, keep separate:

1. legal and governance boundary;
2. procedure family;
3. purchase archetype;
4. purchase execution channel;
5. system support;
6. procurement workflow design for each alternative;
7. contract design for each alternative;
8. organisational implementation readiness.

The compared alternatives are `formalSequential` and
`adaptiveCompliant`. The Tunnel and Field metaphor is secondary explanatory
language. It is not an input or a maturity scale.

System ownership does not establish that controls are configured or used.
Readiness responses are recorded independently and never change `deltaCost`.

## Quantitative contract

For alternative `j` and declared range case `r`:

`total_j,r = roleCost_j,r + nonLabourCost_j,r + delayCost_j,r + contractCost_j,r`

Elapsed time comes from the critical path through the alternative's directed
acyclic process map. Role effort and non-labour costs are summed across all
steps.

The signed comparison is:

`deltaCost = total_formalSequential,central - total_adaptiveCompliant,central`

The outer range is:

`[formal.low - adaptive.high, formal.high - adaptive.low]`

Positive and negative values are both admissible. Swapping alternatives reverses
the sign. The model does not set a quota on either side of zero.

Low, central and high values are declared scenario cases, not statistical
confidence intervals. All mandatory legal waits are fixed, sourced and
identical in both alternatives.

## Monetisation boundary

Native model 2.3 includes:

- role cost;
- non-labour process cost;
- delay cost;
- a bounded competition-transfer stress where competitive access is declared to
  differ and the restricted alternative is identified.

Contract-amendment and TCO differentials start at zero. Informal process bypass
is disclosed but not monetised. Those boundaries prevent qualitative evidence
from being converted into an unsupported monetary effect.

Szucs (2024) supports only the bounded competition price channel under an
explicit population-transfer assumption. It does not estimate the effect of
workflow design for Polish procurement. Official examples from California,
OECD, UZP and the European Commission support the existence of specific
mechanisms, not numerical ProcuraCost ranges.

Values migrated from model 2.2.2 are labelled
`retained_legacy_assumption`. Their retention supports reproducibility, not
empirical status.

The first five reference scenarios use mechanism-specific maps introduced in
model 2.3. Their aggregate base-day totals are retained from 2.2.2, but step
order, day allocation and role-hour allocation are illustrative model inputs.
Retained system-support profiles are applied to those new allocations. Official
cases support only the qualitative mechanisms and do not determine any of these
numbers.

## Conditions represented by the scenarios

### A plausible adaptive mechanism

- ERP transformation discovery: incomplete requirements can create a role for
  problem definition and modular contracting.
- Logistics service redesign: market engagement can test service levels, data
  interfaces and allocation of operational risk.
- Public IT with preliminary market consultation: market learning can precede
  an open procedure while statutory tender and standstill periods remain
  unchanged.
- Discovery and solution co-design: learning and re-scoping can add calendar
  time and staff effort rather than remove it.

### No separate workflow mechanism

- A stable standard service can require the same relevant work in both
  alternatives.
- A catalogue call-off can use identical maps after the framework and catalogue
  are established.
- An MRP release can use identical maps under an existing agreement and
  approved material data.

Catalogue and MRP are neutral controls. Equal maps produce equal central
results. The stable-service starting scenario is a topology control only. It
separately compares open policy-qualified competition with a restricted
shortlist or incumbent continuation and identifies the adaptive/compliant
starting alternative as restricted. That allocation can be reversed or removed
and is not a property of adaptive work. The scenario is therefore not a neutral
total-cost control. These examples define conditions to investigate, not
recommended routes.

## Canonical hypotheses

The following hypotheses govern the three-article cycle.

- **H1a:** Greater ex ante workflow prescriptiveness is associated with more
  elapsed days from approved need to contract signature, conditional on
  purchase complexity, legal boundary, procedure family and the applicable
  mandatory-wait days. **H1b:** it is associated with more buyer, legal and
  manager coordination hours per procedure under the same controls.
- **H2:** Broader ex ante market access is associated with more valid bids and
  a lower award price relative to a preregistered independent benchmark.
- **H3:** Greater ex ante rigidity of contract-change clauses is associated
  with more formal amendments per contract-year. Conditional on clause design,
  the association with workflow gate count should remain within a
  preregistered equivalence bound.
- **H4:** For requirements classified as incomplete before process design,
  adaptive discovery is associated with more pre-solicitation role effort but
  fewer post-solicitation clarification and rework days than a sequential
  design. Both interactions should be absent or remain within preregistered
  equivalence bounds for requirements classified as stable.
- **H5:** A higher logged completion rate for required system controls is
  associated with fewer off-system approvals or orders and fewer missing audit
  events. System ownership and readiness responses are not substitutes for
  logged control use.

Article 1 uses propositions P1a, P1b and P2 to P5. Article 3 operationalises
H1a, H1b and H2 to H5. Both must map one-to-one to this set.

### Crosswalk

| Canonical hypothesis | Article 1 | Primary measure | Empirical treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1a/H1b | P1a/P1b | log cycle days from approved need to contract signature; buyer, legal and manager coordination hours | separate directional outcomes with ex ante workflow exposure and organisation/category/legal-procedure controls, including applicable mandatory-wait days |
| H2 | P2 | market-access design, valid bids, price against an independent ex ante benchmark | directional competition models; bids and benchmark-relative price are outcomes |
| H3 | P3 | clause rigidity, amendments per contract-year, workflow gate count | directional clause model plus conditional gate-count equivalence test |
| H4 | P4 | pre-solicitation role hours; post-solicitation clarification and rework days | preregistered interaction with ex ante requirement incompleteness; stable-requirement equivalence test |
| H5 | P5 | required-control completion, off-system approvals or orders, missing audit events | directional logged-use model; ownership and readiness responses are not substitutes |

Minimum substantively important effects, equivalence bounds, estimators and
exclusion rules belong in the preregistered empirical plan. They are not
properties of the calculator.

## What the current service cannot test

The service cannot establish H3 because native starting scenarios use zero
contract-amendment differentials and do not estimate clause effects.

It cannot establish H5 because the readiness self-description is independent and the
cost model does not observe control utilisation or process bypass.

It also cannot validate a causal net delta from one observed event. That would
require a credible counterfactual for the alternative map and an independently
defensible daily cost of delay.

These are deliberate interpretation boundaries, not evidence that the
hypotheses are true or false.
