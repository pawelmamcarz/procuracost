# Scholarly citation review

Dalsze ustalenia po PR #38: [SOURCE_FOLLOWUP.md](SOURCE_FOLLOWUP.md).
Poniższy raport zachowuje stan poprzedniego przeglądu.


Review date: **21 September 2026**. Model context: **ProcuraCost 2.3.0**.

Scope: the eleven unique scholarly articles, research reports and books named below in `RESEARCH.md` and doctoral Articles 1, 2 and 3. Article 2 was reviewed but was not edited. Official cases, legal sources, code and practitioner/video material are outside this scholarly review. EC2011 is recorded separately because an existing historical conformity assertion needs qualification.

The review checked bibliographic metadata, the passages supporting the associated claims, and effect units where relevant. “Full text accessible” means relevant sections and tables were inspected, not that every page was read. Author manuscripts and preprints are distinguished from published versions. Search-indexed excerpts do not count as a complete reading. This review does not replicate any study or validate its identification assumptions independently.

## EC2011 historical assertion outside the freshly verified matrix

`RESEARCH.md` §3.3 previously presented a historical model 2.2.2 audit's claim that authority-side person-days in the former `pzp_eu` template conformed to the distribution in *Public procurement in Europe: Cost and effectiveness* (European Commission, 2011; PwC, London Economics and Ecorys).

That comparison was **not freshly verified** in this scholarly review. The exact supporting report table and page locator remain **unresolved**. No table number, report link or numerical comparison is supplied as verified evidence here. RESEARCH now records the statement as historical provenance rather than confirmed calibration support. Resolving it requires the original report, the exact authority-side measure and table/page, and the historical template calculation used in the comparison. It cannot validate native model 2.3 inputs.

## Model boundary and outstanding evidence

The review changes source interpretation and documentation only. It does not change model economics: the 2/6/9% competition-transfer stress remains declared, contract-amendment and TCO differentials remain zero, and informal bypass remains non-monetised.

Outstanding limits are the Beuve prose/table discrepancy and unavailable final full text, original-edition book access, incomplete final-version access for the author-manuscript checks noted above, and the EC2011 historical report locator. Bibliographic existence, thematic relevance and access to a manuscript do not independently establish causal validity or transferability to Polish procurement workflows.
