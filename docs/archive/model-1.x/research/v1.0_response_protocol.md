# v1.0 Response Protocol (Private)

> **Historical artifact:** retained for project history; current review work targets model v1.2.

**Goal**: When feedback arrives, follow a disciplined, traceable process that protects the core strengths of the v1.0 package (especially the 2×2 as first-class and the reproducibility infrastructure) while remaining open to improvement.

## High-Level Sequence

1. **Receive & Log**
   - Save all feedback (emails, marked-up PDFs, notes) in one place.
   - Immediately enter every comment into `docs/research/v1.0_feedback_log.md` (use the pre-populated structure).
   - Note date received and source.

2. **Initial Triage (same day or next day)**
   - Read everything once for overall tone and major themes.
   - Do a quick pass to flag:
     - Comments that touch our red lines (see `v1.0_red_lines.md`)
     - Comments that match anticipated concerns (see `v1.0_anticipated_concerns.md`)
   - Do **not** decide changes yet.

3. **Consolidated Summary (within 1 week of receiving all expected feedback)**
   - Use `docs/research/v1.0_feedback_summary_template.md`
   - Group by area (2×2, Reproducibility, Propositions, Limitations, Narrative/Other).
   - Prioritize: High / Medium / Low.
   - For each high/medium item, record:
     - Reviewer's concern
     - Our initial reaction
     - Cross-reference to red lines or anticipated concerns
     - Proposed decision (Accept / Partial / Decline + rationale)

4. **Decision Meeting (internal)**
   - Review the summary together (or with 1-2 trusted people if available).
   - For every red-line item: explicitly decide whether to defend, reframe, or (rarely) concede.
   - Produce a final prioritized change list.

5. **Implementation**
   - Make changes in a dedicated branch or clearly marked v1.1 section.
   - Keep changes minimal and targeted where possible.
   - For any change that affects quantitative claims or multipliers, note the exact before/after.

6. **Re-Verification**
   - Run the full `docs/research/v1.0_post_feedback_checklist.md`
   - In particular:
     - Re-compute affected multipliers and spot-check at least one Direct+Upstream export.
     - Update any affected sample data or notes in `replication/`.
     - Verify that the reproducibility story (3.8 recipe, statement, replication subsection) still holds.

7. **Documentation & Communication**
   - Update the self-review notes with what was changed and why.
   - If appropriate, prepare a short "changes since v1.0 review draft" note (can be sent back to reviewers if they want to see how their input was used).
   - Update main status files (RESEARCH.md, app/research/page.tsx, trackers) to v1.1.

8. **Decide Next Actions**
   - Is the paper now strong enough to submit or to send to more reviewers?
   - Do we need to expand the reviewer list?
   - Time to start the slide deck?

## Principles During Incorporation

- Protect the core: 2×2 as first-class, reproducibility as a genuine strength, propositions tied to the implementation.
- Be generous with reframing but stingy with weakening the central claims.
- Every material change to a number or strong claim must be re-verified with a fresh export.
- Document decisions so we can explain them later (to reviewers, collaborators, or ourselves in 6 months).
